## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, grounded approach to personal and household financial clarity. Balancing architectural precision with the warmth of a heritage lifestyle publication, the aesthetic dispels standard fintech anxiety in favor of quiet control, longevity, and domestic order. 

The target audience spans discerning heads of households, dual-income partners, and property owners seeking a unified, dignified operating system for their physical and financial assets. The visual tone is structured, calm, human, and meticulously documented.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Editorial Structure:** High-contrast typographic scale paired with quiet, tactile negative space evokes the pacing of an archival journal.
- **Architectural Grounding:** Heavy forest baselines stabilize delicate warm off-white canvas surfaces, using structural dividing rules instead of artificial container clutter.
- **Human Warmth:** Ochre and terracotta accents punctuate analytical clarity with life, human agency, and domestic warmth.

## Colors

The palette pairs botanical forestry with warm earthen clays and bleached paper whites, establishing structural stability without corporate sterility.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#FBFBF9`):** A soft, warm off-white surface that eliminates eye fatigue and reads like archival rag paper.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white reserved for modular data cards, metrics containers, and active input regions.
- **Surface Sunken (`#F5F5F0`):** Secondary nested wells, table headers, and structural tab tracks.
- **Deep Contrast Slate (`#0F1F1C`):** Used for anchor callout modules, key feature focus blocks, and the primary footer to weight the composition.

### Functional Accents
- **Primary Brand (`#1E3A34` & `#132A25`):** Deep spruce slate serving as the grounding spine for titles, primary actions, and key structural frames.
- **Warm Ochre / Terracotta (`#D97746` & `#C46231`):** Action indicators, interactive highlights, urgent household actions, and primary interactive conversions.
- **Sage Eucalyptus (`#4D7C6F`, `#DCE9E4`, `#EBF3F0`):** Reassuring growth states, paid balances, verified maintenance records, and secondary interactive chips.
- **Editorial Linework (`#E2E8E5`):** Razor-thin dividers ensuring structural division without visual density.

## Typography

The type system balances the authority of classic print literature with the modern utility demanded by digital ledger management.

- **Headlines (Newsreader):** Brings a refined, intellectual presence to marketing statements, section markers, and hero hooks. It signals bespoke craftsmanship rather than mass-market utility. Italic variations are reserved strictly for emotional emphasis keywords (e.g., *clarity*, *sanctuary*, *peace*).
- **Body & Numerical Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly geometry and exceptional clarity in data-dense compositions. Monospaced tabular numerals must be enabled via `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for all financial tables, cash flow lists, and property valuation figures.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase with open tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) to frame content categories cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column layout grid structures content with calm, predictable rhythm. Layouts rely on architectural boundaries rather than arbitrary card nests.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, max-width 1280px, centered canvas, 24px gutters, 48px outer canvas margin.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, fluid width, 20px gutters, 32px outer canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, fluid width, 16px gutters, 20px outer canvas margin.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections should be separated by rhythmically generous spaces (80px to 120px on desktop, 48px to 64px on mobile) to maintain an unhurried, editorial reading pace.
- Form inputs, tabular ledgers, and list rows maintain tight micro-spacing (`space-xs` through `space-md`) to ensure high visual utility and scanning speed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic drop shadows, relying on layered surface tones, precise hairline borders, and subtle diffuse contact shading.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#FBFBF9` provides the bottom physical layer.
- **Framed Containers:** Elements rest on `#FFFFFF` elevated surfaces bordered by a 1px solid hairline (`#E2E8E5`).
- **Tactile Depth (Cards & Previews):** Interactive previews and floating modules use an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow:
  - `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 31, 28, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 31, 28, 0.06)`
- **Active / Dragged Overlays:** Raised state uses a deeper, warm-tinted shadow:
  - `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(15, 31, 28, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(15, 31, 28, 0.04)`
- **Inverted Blocks:** Dark containers (`#0F1F1C`) create intentional depth through contrast rather than elevation, visually anchoring sections like the product mockups and final conversion blocks.

## Shapes

The design system maintains tailored geometry that avoids both cold, razor-sharp brutalism and bubbly, toy-like interfaces.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to buttons, inputs, segmented controls, chips, and small interactive elements.
- **Large Surface Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to standard modular feature cards, dashboard preview panels, and drawer shells.
- **Hero & Shell Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for major content blocks, inverted hero containers, and application mockup canvases.
- **Badges & Micro Pills:** Fully rounded (9999px) strictly for status pills and category markers to distinguish metadata from actionable controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E3A34`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 8px. Hover shifts to `#132A25` with a subtle transform lift (`translateY(-1px)`). Focus ring: 2px solid `#D97746` offset 2px.
- **Accent (Conversion CTA):** Background `#D97746`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts to `#C46231`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#1E3A34`, text `#1E3A34`. Hover shifts to `#EBF3F0`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#1E3A34`, zero border. Hover shifts to `#F5F5F0`.

### Cards & UI Mockup Frames
- **Editorial Cards:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border (`#E2E8E5`), 16px radius, internal padding `space-lg` to `space-xl`. 
- **Application Mockup Canvases:** Surface tinted `#FFFFFF` within a deep forest frame or soft canvas shadow. Metric grids within mockups feature clear dividing rules (`border-b border-[#E2E8E5]`) with monospace tabular numbers for asset values, cash flow rates, and maintenance timelines.

### Interactive FAQ Accordion
- Borderless rows separated by a 1px solid `#E2E8E5` line.
- Header button spans full width with Newsreader serif question text (`headline-sm`).
- Expand indicator is a clean geometric plus/minus glyph that smoothly rotates 45 degrees upon expansion.
- Answer panel renders in `body-md` using slate text (`#526460`) with deliberate bottom padding (`space-lg`).

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8E5`, 8px radius, text `#1A2421`, placeholder `#526460`. Active focus creates a 1px solid `#1E3A34` outline.
- **Segmented Switcher (Module Tabs):** Enclosed pill track in `#F5F5F0` with a 4px inner gutter. Active tab slides with a white `#FFFFFF` surface, 6px radius, and subtle micro-shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Badges:** Height 24px, pill-shaped, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
  - *Financial Positive / Health:* Background `#EBF3F0`, text `#1E3A34`.
  - *Attention / Urgent Task:* Background `#FDF0EA`, text `#C46231`.
  - *Neutral Metric:* Background `#F5F5F0`, text `#526460`.
